# Starting-position evidence audit

## Decision status

The supplied rulebook confirms that there are 18 start cards and that their numbers determine pawn starting positions. The available physical card photograph is incomplete and uncontrolled, so it cannot verify the full 18-value starting-position set.

No starting-position dataset has been added to canonical board data.

## Primary rulebook evidence

The supplied rulebook PDF provides these facts:

- Page 2 lists `18 start cards` among the game contents.
- Page 4 instructs players to mix the 18 numbered cards face down and draw one card for each pawn controlled.
- Page 4 says the numbers determine the starting positions for the pawns.
- Page 4 gives `198` as an example starting card near the Oval Cricket Ground.

The rulebook does not print the complete 18-card number list.

## Photograph assessment

Source: `D:\03_Development\GameDev\scotland-yard-docs\photoscans\starting cards.jpeg` (registered as IMG-03 in the evidence register).

The photograph shows a casual spread of blue cards with several readable faces, but it has these limitations:

- cards are rotated in different directions;
- several cards are cropped by the image frame or overlap the frame edge;
- the full count of 18 cards cannot be established from visible card boundaries;
- there is no controlled card-by-card inventory or edition/product panel;
- some faces are too small or partially obscured to transcribe safely.

## What is missing

To verify all 18 starting positions, capture either:

1. all 18 cards face-up in one flat, glare-free image with every edge visible and values legible; or
2. one controlled image per card, preserving the card back/front relationship and target-edition provenance.

The capture should also include the product/copyright panel or be tied to a separately verified `4500-X2` component set. The final list must be independently counted, deduplicated, and compared with the physical board markers before entering canonical data.

## Current conclusion

The evidence establishes the existence of 18 start cards and records 13 safely legible card-face values. It does not verify all 18 positions, their complete list, or the allocation of any card to a player. Starting-position canonicalization remains blocked.
