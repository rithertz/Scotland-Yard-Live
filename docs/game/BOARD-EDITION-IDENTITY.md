# Board edition identity audit

## Decision status

The supplied rulebook is identified with high confidence as the 1985 Milton Bradley edition manufactured under licence by Funskool (India) Ltd., product code `4500-X2`. The photographed board is a probable match, but the accessible board photographs do not contain a sufficiently legible copyright/product panel to prove that the physical board and rulebook are the same manufactured copy or edition variant.

This document records source evidence only. It does not create canonical station, edge, coordinate, or starting-position data.

## Rulebook facts relevant to identity and inventory

- Page 2 lists 18 start cards and one gameboard map of Central London.
- Page 4 explains that the 18 start-card numbers determine pawn starting positions.
- Page 5 describes numbered locations as stations or stops and explains the yellow, green, and red service signals.
- Page 5 states that all numbered locations have taxi service, while service colours identify bus and underground availability.
- Page 10 explicitly describes Mr X's boat travel from `194` to `157`, `157` to `115`, and `115` to `118`, in either direction.
- Page 12 provides the edition and manufacturer identifiers above.

The rulebook does not state “199 locations,” “200 locations,” or any other explicit board station count in the reviewed pages. Its use of numbered locations and start cards does not, by itself, resolve the status of the photographed `200` marker.

## Identity conclusion

The strongest defensible identity statement is:

> The supplied rulebook is the 1985 Milton Bradley / Funskool India `4500-X2` rulebook. The supplied board photographs are consistent with that target, but the exact identity of the photographed board remains unconfirmed because its product/copyright panel is not legible in the available images.

The project should retain the approved 1985 / Funskool / `4500-X2` target decision while treating the board-photo match as probable rather than confirmed. No external edition, online map, or third-party graph is used to fill the gap.

## Evidence needed to close identity

Capture a separate, straight-on, glare-free, high-resolution photograph of the physical board's copyright/product panel and any box/rulebook identity panel. The capture should preserve the surrounding board border so the panel can be tied to the photographed board rather than merely to a loose component.
